## Accepted ranking scope

The pure library ranks the complete supplied typed P07 candidate snapshot by
proved height and full structural key, committed-input weighted native depth,
or a source-cardinality tuple/index-slot proxy. It retains the original P06
verified optimum and checks every candidate's required height and exact
catalog/query binding before scoring. Statistical values do not supply proof
authority. An unfinished candidate family remains explicitly a retained prefix.

Actual PhysicalRuntime, DeltaRuntime and QuerySession bridges capture bounded
owner facts and revalidate the actual owner/layer/committed sequence, workload
interval and mode/configuration generations. Retained storage invalidation stamps
are separate diagnostics and do not define owner freshness. Current physical
inputs include no-ops and each alias occurrence
contributes its own native depth. Unused physical tables, unknown/stale/missing
facts, zero input denominators, source limits and fallback reasons stay visible.
Policies retain their complete recorded inputs, unreduced exact scores and
per-candidate terms. Independent hot-source, alias, empty-input, binding,
freshness and arithmetic fixtures reproduce these decisions.

The slot estimate is a logical tuple/index-record bound, not allocator bytes,
peak memory, calibrated maintenance cost or an S05 accounting result. Limits
bound the whole retained candidate set, preparation and ranking; exhaustion
publishes no partial winner. Optional height fallback applies only to the
documented unavailable-statistics conditions. Allocation/processing/time stops
do not become fallback. Finite native lowering/replay limits and explicit
cumulative soft timing remain in the [accounting guide](../ranking_accounting.md).
The [API guide](../ranking.md) includes separately compiled production/testing
examples. No ranking CLI or automatic plan deployment was added.
