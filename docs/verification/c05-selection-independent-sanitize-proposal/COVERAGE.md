The byte consumer has a separate framed-byte oracle and explicit mutation

or semantic Evidence authority. The neutral private Budget test likewise uses

The real two-assessment resource fixture is DirectCQ. Its exact/one-under loop
checks every additive domain whose observed usage is nonzero; zero counters
are explicitly skipped. In particular, this is not an independent real
normalized replay/normalization exact/one-under boundary test. The producer's
source consumer checks real normalized lowering, identity, binding-replay and
normalization caps at zero, and its normalized persistent-failure fixture
exercises the real lowering/replay/identity/publication path. Its separate
exact/one-under resource loop is also DirectCQ. Those complementary checks do
not fill the narrow real normalized exact/one-under gap. Their execution
receipts must be read on their own terms, rather than attributed to these
independent consumers.

The focused failure consumer measures success first and probes selected early,
middle and final allocation positions for cold eviction and warm-hit current
publication. It checks owner/workload state, complete cache order/counters,
retained results and retry behavior. It complements the producer's full
persistent-position sweeps; it does not duplicate or replace every position.
Any recovered completion after publication is reported separately and still
must satisfy the complete result/counter assertions.

No full-field saturation, exhaustive normalized binding, all compiler policies,
total allocator/peak-memory bounds or elapsed-time performance claim follows
from this packet. Tuple/index slots and declared payload bytes remain C02/C05
logical proxies, not S05 memory predictions. The author previously authored the
accepted C02 independent public/integer fixtures and discloses that lineage;
the author did not write the ranking or selection implementation bodies.
