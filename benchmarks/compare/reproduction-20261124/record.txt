Scope
-----
This record covers Claude's comparison matrix in build/experiments/compare-v1
and the subsequent native optimization follow-ups. The matrix has 34 paper
replay points in full-snapshot and per-update-delta modes. It covers eight
graph queries and the SNB workloads and scales specified by that campaign.
The eight graph queries also have a maintenance-only point. A delta point
retains full snapshots at markers where its input binding requests them.

The historical matrix had 638 completed jobs: 375 C++ CROWN, 213 Flink,
48 F-IVM and two exact verification calls. The archived Flink p1 series
supplies ten runs per paper point, including the 25 points where the later
campaign skipped an additional p1 repeat. The p1 archive is kept as its
own cohort. Each of the 34 points has three p4 and three p16 campaign runs.

The final saved C++ binaries are compare-v1/bin-fivm3. Claude's later
measurements covered selected cases on this build. The final-cxx replay uses
these binaries across the entire historical C++ matrix, including three
repetitions per GC configuration. It keeps the original 256 MiB tiers and
adds any tiers required by the same first-run RSS rule. The original run
files, executable builds and reports are preserved.

Cohorts and metrics
-------------------
CROWN wall time comes from GNU time for each process, including teardown.
Blocking GC uses one configured core. Background GC uses two configured
cores. F-IVM is the native C++ implementation with the signed factorized ring
and index fixes recorded in benchmarks/fivm_replay/record.txt. F-IVM full mode
visits snapshot rows. Its maintenance-only runs are paired with the CROWN
none points. F-IVM has no per-update output in this campaign.

Flink wall time comes from its launcher receipt. The internal timer is the
maximum AccumulateTime across workers. The original and final C++ experiments
ran concurrent jobs on disjoint physical cores. The final C++ replay had no
concurrent Flink jobs. The archived p1 trials and targeted A/B trials had their
own schedules. Timing comparisons across these cohorts include the effects
of those different conditions.

The audit checks successful receipt status, executed configurations, elapsed
time and resource records, and archived Flink stdout/stderr/RSS fingerprints.
It also compares native input, relation update, delta and snapshot counts
and marker signatures across the C++ builds. Existing row-by-row checks on
the sliding-window and long prefixes are reused for byte-identical binaries.
The final fallback was independently checked on both dumbbell queries.
F-IVM full runs are checked against finite-input snapshot oracles, with
separate fixture-based row validation in its own experiment record.

Replay commands
---------------
Run from the CROWN root with the archived inputs, original measurements and
the four saved binaries available. Use a fresh replay directory.

  old=build/experiments/compare-v1
  replay=build/experiments/compare-reproduction-fresh
  out=build/experiments/compare-reproduction-fresh-report
  python3 benchmarks/compare/prepare_reproduction.py "$old" "$replay"
  python3 benchmarks/compare/scheduler.py "$replay" --poll 1
  python3 benchmarks/compare/collect_reproduction.py "$old" "$replay" "$out" --verify-inputs

The collector can export a progress snapshot with --allow-unfinished. A
final audit records complete=true only after all planned measurement receipts
are accepted and both matrix coverage checks pass.
